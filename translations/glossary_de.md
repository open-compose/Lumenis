# Cutthulhu German (de) glossary

Terms as German-speaking editors use them in NLE software. English loanwords are kept where the German
editing world uses them (Clip, Bin, Timeline, Rendern, Keyframe, Trim). Nouns take German capitalisation and
gender noted where useful.
